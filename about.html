The Vision

A Peaceful Sanctuary in Puri

At Hotel Deep Ganga, we believe that every guest is a blessing. Located in the serene Baliapanda area, our hotel was established to provide a peaceful sanctuary for pilgrims visiting the Jagannath Temple and tourists seeking the therapeutic breeze of the Bay of Bengal.

Our name draws inspiration from the sacred rivers of India — "Deep" meaning light, and "Ganga" symbolizing purity and life. We strive to be a beacon of warmth and comfort in the holy city of Puri.
